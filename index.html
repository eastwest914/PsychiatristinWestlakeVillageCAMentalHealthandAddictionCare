Frequently Asked Questions

What does a psychiatrist do?

A psychiatrist is a medical professional who evaluates mental health conditions, makes a diagnosis, and may recommend therapy, medication or a combination of both.


What is an intensive outpatient program (IOP)?

An IOP is a structured treatment program that provides several hours of therapy and support each week while you continue living at home. It offers more support than weekly therapy without residential care.


Do you treat alcohol and drug addiction?

We provide outpatient and IOP-level programs for alcohol and drug use. Call us to talk through your situation and find out whether our programs suit your needs.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept insurance, credit cards and cash. Please call to confirm your insurance coverage.

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

An intensive outpatient program is designed for people who need more than weekly appointments but do not need to live in a facility. Sessions are scheduled several times a week.

The IOP program builds coping skills, accountability and support, and it can be a step down from a higher level of care or a step up from standard therapy.
